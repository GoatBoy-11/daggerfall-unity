Argonian staff fighter / quarterstaff monk in a red top, with a rigged tail

Manual spawn: anpc_spawn argonian_staff_fighter_1
Female Argonian, Monk class, calm (fights back when attacked), Brave, wander
radius 8. No spells. Argonian names (argonian list); custom portrait.
Spawns in cities, hamlets and villages (0-1 each) and sometimes in temples and
Fighters Guild halls (10%). Needs Advanced NPCs with "race": "Argonian" support.

14 sheets, 8 directions, 8 fps, 256-pixel cells; sprites.json carries
"standingHeight" (body only). Fights with a two-handed quarterstaff: a low
guard with the rear fist at her right hip; attack_1 is an overhead strike
(staff raised high, brought down on the head), attack_2 a lunging thrust
with the front end; both land on frame 3. She walks and runs with the staff
held level at her hips, and talks, greets and casts with it planted upright.
Her tail sways, streams back on the run and drapes to the floor when she dies
face down; the staff falls beside her. Includes hit blood, the growing death
pool and casting effects.

Source: Models/argonian_staff (README there). Same model and rig as
