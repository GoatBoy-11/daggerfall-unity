Cowled old man / stand-in for Daggerfall's cowled old man (flat 177.0)

"replaces": ["177.0"]: he takes the place of every vanilla cowled-old-man flat.
Clicking him opens that NPC's own talk, quests and guild services; the vanilla
face and name are kept (replacePortrait and replaceName false). He stays within
2 m of the flat's spot when calm. No spawn block: he never appears otherwise.
Calm Breton Sorcerer, Prudent: fights back when attacked.

Sheets (8 directions, 8 fps): idle_1 (the vanilla pose: the dragon-wing staff
planted at arm's length, its head pulsing with light), walk (the staff as a
walking stick), run (staff across his body in both hands), greet_1, talk_1,
attack_1 (two-handed overhead strike, actionFrame 3), attack_range_1 (aims the
staff's dragon head and looses a blue fireball, actionFrame 3), cast_1, hit_1,
death_1 (the staff is knocked away; he ends face down). projectile.png: an
8-frame blue fireball (5-11 damage a hit). Spells: Shock, Paralysis, Heal,
Shield (120 magicka). Voice: skpy "raspy", pitched down.

Source: Models/cowled_old_man (README there). Models: the user's Meshy "The
Amethyst Archmage" and "Golden Dragon Wing Staff"; the fireball is procedural.
