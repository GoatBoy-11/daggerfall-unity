Advanced NPCs example sounds
============================

All clips were converted to mono 22 kHz OGG, trimmed, peak-normalized (most) and renamed; Rookie's combined damage and
effort recordings were cut into single sounds. Clips from CC BY-SA packs stay under CC BY-SA 4.0.
