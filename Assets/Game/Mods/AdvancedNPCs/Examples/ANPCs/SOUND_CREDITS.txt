Advanced NPCs example sounds
============================

Voice lines (greet, death, talk, and the wench's hit sounds) are from the Super Dialogue Audio Pack V1 by Dillon Becker
(dillonbecker.com), licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: chosen lines
converted to mono 22 kHz OGG and renamed.

Attack and hit voices (NOX_SOUND Voices Essentials) and footsteps (NOX_SOUND Footsteps Essentials; the default steps in
_Sounds are its DirtyGround walk steps, as orc_weakling's) are by Nox Sound,
released under CC0. Changes: converted to mono 22 kHz OGG and renamed.

Talk sounds (conversations between ANPCs) are confirmations, refusals and murmurs from the same Super Dialogue
Audio Pack V1, each example keeping the actor of its greetings (wench: Karen Cenon, argonian_female_acrobat_1: Meghan
Christian, castle_guard_1: Sean Lenhart, dunmer_citizen_1: Alex Brodie, khajiit_citizen_1: Ian Lampert). breton_nightblade
(Alex Brodie), dunmer_mercenary and ohmes_knight_1 (Ian Lampert) and iron_sentinel (Sean Lenhart) use another mix of
the same actors' confirmations, refusals and murmurs, at a different pitch.
The talk sounds of argonian_staff_fighter_1 (female V1), dunmer_spellblade and nord_barbarian_1 (male V1), and
orc_weakling and temple_monk_1 (male V2) are nods, thinking sounds, short laughs, sighs and surprise from NOX_SOUND
Voices Essentials (CC0). Changes: converted to mono 22 kHz OGG, peak-normalized and renamed.
The templates' talk sounds use the same two packs: baker (NOX female V2), cooper (Sean Lenhart), smith (Alex Brodie),
bandit (NOX male V1 / female V1), commoner (Ian Lampert / Karen Cenon), patron (Alex Brodie / Meghan Christian) and
spellcaster (NOX male V2 / female V2), male / female for the templates whose gender is picked at random.
