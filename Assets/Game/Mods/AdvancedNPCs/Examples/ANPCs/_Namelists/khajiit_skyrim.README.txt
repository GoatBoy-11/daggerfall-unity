khajiit_skyrim

200 original Skyrim-style Khajiit whole names: 100 male and 100 female.
Includes varied short names and apostrophe-prefixed names. No separate
surnames are added. These are invented names, not a list of named Skyrim
characters or a dictionary of translated Khajiit titles.

Use "nameList": "khajiit_skyrim" in any ANPC's npc.json.
